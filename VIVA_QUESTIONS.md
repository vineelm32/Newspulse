# Viva Questions and Short Answers

**1. What is NewsPulse?** A data science app that estimates sentiment in news text and summarizes patterns by category and date.

**2. What is sentiment analysis?** An NLP task that estimates text polarity, often positive, neutral, or negative.

**3. Why use Pandas?** To load, clean, filter, group, and summarize data.

**4. What is VADER?** A lexicon- and rule-based sentiment tool that returns positive, neutral, negative, and compound scores.

**5. What is TextBlob polarity?** A score generally between -1 and +1 estimating text polarity.

**6. Is a sentiment score a probability?** No, not automatically.

**7. How are charts created?** Pandas aggregates results; Matplotlib and Streamlit display them.

**8. What is a weekly report?** A summary of records in a selected seven-day period.

**9. Does sentiment analysis detect fake news?** No. It estimates language polarity and does not verify claims.

**10. How can accuracy be measured?** Compare predictions with known reference labels using appropriate metrics.

**11. What are limitations?** Sarcasm, context, ambiguous wording, dataset bias, and method choice can affect predictions.

**12. What are future enhancements?** RSS ingestion, labeled-data evaluation, contextual NLP, automated reports, and deployment.
