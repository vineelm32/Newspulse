# Results and Limitations

The app reports article counts, sentiment prediction counts and percentages, category-wise summaries, date trends, and weekly summaries. These are descriptive summaries, not model accuracy.

They do not prove prediction accuracy, verify article claims, or directly measure public opinion.

For academic evaluation, obtain labeled data, compare predictions with reference labels, report accuracy/precision/recall/F1 and a confusion matrix, and discuss errors.

**Current status:** lexicon-based scoring is implemented. Supervised model training and automated live RSS ingestion are not implemented. Add measured values only after a documented evaluation.
